Frequently Asked Questions About Phones & Tablets

What is the best phone to buy in Egypt in 2026?
The best phone depends on your budget. Under EGP 7,000, the Infinix Smart 10 and Samsung Galaxy A07 lead on value. Between EGP 7,000–10,000, the XIAOMI Redmi 15C and Samsung Galaxy A17 are the most-bought models. For flagship buyers, the iPhone 17 Pro Max and Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra are the top picks on Jumia Egypt.
How much does an iPhone cost in Egypt?
iPhone prices in Egypt on Jumia start at around EGP 30,000 for older models and reach EGP 90,000+ for the iPhone 17 Pro Max. Prices vary with storage (128 GB, 256 GB, 512 GB, 1 TB) and seller. Jumia Mall verified sellers and Dream 2000 carry the official Apple-warranty stock.
Does Jumia Egypt offer Cash on Delivery for phones?

Can I buy a phone in installments in Egypt without a credit card?

Yes. valU, Aman, Souhoola and Forsa let you buy in installments without a credit card — only your national ID is required for approval. Approval typically takes minutes. valU is the most widely used option on Jumia Egypt.

How long is the warranty on phones from Jumia?

Most phones on Jumia Egypt carry a 12-month manufacturer warranty serviced through the brand's official Egyptian agents (Apple via authorised partners, Samsung Egypt, Xiaomi Mi Store, OPPO Egypt, Honor Egypt). The warranty period and coverage are listed on each product page.

What is the cheapest 5G phone on Jumia Egypt?

The most affordable 5G phones on Jumia start around EGP 8,000–10,000, typically from Xiaomi, OPPO and Honor. The OPPO Reno 15F (5G) and Samsung Galaxy A56 5G are popular mid-range 5G options between EGP 20,000–25,000.
